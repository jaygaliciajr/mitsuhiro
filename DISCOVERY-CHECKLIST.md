# Discovery checklist — for the owner of Hiromitsu

Everything below is missing from the website. Nothing on the site invents any
of it, which is why several pages currently read as blank.

The site renders this same list at `/owner-checklist`, generated from
`src/content/business.ts`, so the two cannot drift apart.

## Before anything else

These four unblock the pages that matter most to a customer.

- [ ] **Menu.** The current menu with dish names, short descriptions and
      prices. Say which items are always available and which come and go.
- [ ] **What the kitchen serves.** One sentence describing the food. Nothing
      about the cuisine appears on the site until this exists.
- [ ] **Opening hours.** Each day, plus holiday closures and last order times.
- [ ] **How people order.** Walk in only? Phone ahead? A delivery app? The site
      will not promise a channel you do not actually run.

## Menu detail

- [ ] **Prices**, and whether they include tax and service.
- [ ] **Reservations** — do you take them, for what group sizes, how far ahead?
- [ ] **Delivery** — do you deliver, what area, any minimum order?
- [ ] **Payment methods** — cash, cards, e-wallets.

## Visiting

- [ ] **Seating, parking and access.** Roughly how many you seat, whether there
      is outdoor seating or parking, and whether there is step-free access.
      Accessibility is one thing the site will never guess at.

## Contact

- [ ] **Public email address**, and who monitors it. The message form on the
      contact page stays switched off until this exists — a form with nowhere
      to deliver is worse than no form.
- [ ] **Official Facebook page URL**, if there is one. A page found by
      searching your name is not proof it is yours.
- [ ] **Privacy contact** — the person or inbox that handles data requests, and
      how long you want enquiry messages kept.
- [ ] **Expected reply time** for written messages, if you want one stated.

## Brand

- [ ] **Registered business name**, if it differs from "Hiromitsu".
- [ ] **The story** — two or three sentences in your own words. Who runs the
      place, how long it has been open, what you want people to know.
- [ ] **Logo** as SVG or high-resolution PNG. Until then the site uses the name
      set in type. No mark will be drawn on your behalf.
- [ ] **Photographs you own and approve** — the room, the counter, the food. No
      stock or AI-generated images will stand in for your restaurant.
- [ ] **Proof you can back up** — awards, licences, press. Nothing goes on the
      site without evidence, and no reviews or ratings will be invented.

## Decisions, not content

- [ ] **Domain.** Which address should this site live at, and who owns it?
- [ ] **Language.** English only, or Filipino as well?
- [ ] **Analytics.** Do you want visitor numbers at all? The site currently
      collects nothing and sets no cookies.
- [ ] **Who signs off.** Name the person who approves the public wording before
      launch.
